ENGINEERING (F2) - Ship status
Reactor load and temperature, shields, hull, airlocks, umbilicals, tractor beam,
Geiger counter, coolant and life support.
Watch the reactor: overloading causes overheating and explosive failure.

REACTOR
INJECT COOLANT <amount>       Injects coolant (litres) into the reactor
DISPLAY DIAGNOSTICS           Status of all components and fighters

SHIELDS
SET SHIELDS [face] <%>        Shield power (no face = all shields)
ABORT RECHARGE SHIELDS [face] Cancels shield recharging
  [face] = FORWARD | STARBOARD | PORT | AFT (ALL on ABORT)

TRACTOR BEAM
ENABLE TRACTOR BEAM           Turns on (needs a selected target)
DISABLE TRACTOR BEAM          Turns off
REVERSE TRACTOR POLARITY      Switches between ATTRACT and REPEL

EMERGENCY
DISABLE ALL [NOW]             Disables all systems (NOW = no confirmation)
SELF DESTRUCT                 Self-destruct (confirm with Y, 15 s)
ABORT DESTRUCT                Attempts to cancel self-destruct

WARNINGS
- Shields, lasers, engines and scanners all add reactor load.
- WARP puts a massive load on the reactor.
- Radiation above safe levels breaches the shielding (Geiger).
- An open airlock drains life support.
