Quick reference for the modules and commands of Astra Protocol 2.
Unofficial fan tool. Astra Protocol 2 is a game by Slice Bar Games;
commands are based on the official Astral Guidance Operating System manual.

F1–F6       Open the reference screens (same keys as the game)
F7          Coordinate calculator (ship + scan = destination)
F8          Language and about
F9 / Ctrl+F Free search across screens and procedures
Tab         Switch between reference screens and procedures
↑ ↓ ← →     Navigate and scroll
Esc         Go back / exit
