# keywords: drones, squadron, escort, launch, recall, wingmen
USE FIGHTERS
Launch the AI drone fighters, follow them and bring them back.

STEPS
1. SET FIGHTER MODE CLOSEST   Attack the nearest enemy (set before launch)
2. LAUNCH FIGHTERS            Launch from all bays (or one: LAUNCH FIGHTERS 1)
3. DISPLAY FIGHTER STATUS     Follow their status
4. RECALL FIGHTERS            Bring them back (needs a free bay)

MODES
- CLOSEST, FIGHTERS, SHIPS, TARGET, or IDLE (follows the ship, no attack).

WATCH
- Fighters run on limited fuel: recall them before they run out.
- Fighter bays are shown in ENGINEERING (F2); fighters in OPERATIONS (F6).
