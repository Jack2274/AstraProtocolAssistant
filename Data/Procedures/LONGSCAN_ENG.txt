LONG RANGE SURVEY
Find celestial bodies, artificial signals and stations in the sector.

STEPS
1. ENABLE RADIO               Turn on the long range scanners
2. SET RADIO 10000            Scanner range
3. LONG RANGE SCAN            Analyse the sector
4. DISPLAY LONG RANGE SCAN    Show the results
5. SCAN SPACECRAFT            (optional) Nearby ships and their distance

WATCH
- Destination = ship coordinates (HELM) + scan coordinates, whatever the
  ship's heading. F7 (calculator) does the sum and copies PLOT ROUTE.
- Scanners add load to the reactor; DISABLE RADIO when not needed.
