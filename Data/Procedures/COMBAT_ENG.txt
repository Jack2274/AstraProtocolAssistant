ENGAGE IN COMBAT
Prepare the ship and attack a hostile ship.

BEFORE YOU START
- Check reactor load and hull integrity in ENGINEERING (F2).

STEPS
1. SCAN SPACECRAFT            Locate nearby ships
2. SET SHIELDS 100            Shields at full power
3. ENABLE LASERS              Automatic laser fire
4. SET LASER MODE TARGET      Lasers prioritise the current target
5. TARGET SHIP                Lock the nearest ship
6. SCAN                       Target schematics (shown in ORDNANCE)
7. FIRE ALL                   Fire all loaded missiles
8. RELOAD ALL                 Reload before the next volley

OPTIONAL
- ENABLE SIGNAL JAMMERS       Jam the target's signals
- LAUNCH EMP                  Pulse that degrades nearby electronics

WATCH
- Shields and lasers add load to the reactor: watch ENGINEERING (F2).
- Missiles home in on the target; check the ammunition in ORDNANCE (F4).
- The EMP hits every electrical device within the pulse, maybe your own ship:
  do not launch it with the enemy right next to you (default fuse: 3 s).
