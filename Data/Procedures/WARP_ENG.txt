# keywords: jump, teleport, hyperspace, fast travel, far, distance
WARP JUMP
Fold space to reach a distant point almost instantly.

BEFORE YOU START
- Check the reactor in ENGINEERING (F2): the warp puts a massive load on it.

STEPS
1. DISABLE AUTOPILOT          Avoid the autopilot moving the ship
2. STABILIZE ALL              The ship must stay stationary
3. WARP 10,10                 Start the warp drive to the coordinate
4. INJECT COOLANT 20          (if needed) Cool the reactor while charging
5. ABORT WARP                 (if needed) Cancel the jump

WATCH
- The warp takes some time to charge; do not move the ship meanwhile.
- After the jump, an electromagnetic shock may disable systems for a while.
