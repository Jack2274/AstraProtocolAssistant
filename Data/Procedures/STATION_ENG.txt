INVESTIGATE A STATION
Scan a space station, read its data and fly next to it.

BEFORE YOU START
- Open SCIENCE (F3). The station must be within short range scanner distance.

STEPS
1. TARGET STATION             Lock the nearest station
2. SCAN                       Scan the target (or F9)
3. DISPLAY DETAILS            Scientific readout (←/→ pages)
4. PLOT ROUTE TARGET          Route to the station
5. ENABLE AUTOPILOT           Approach the station
6. STABILIZE ALL              Stop next to the station
7. DISPLAY DOCKING HUD        Docking view (only in HELM, F1)

WATCH
- DISPLAY DETAILS only works in SCIENCE, and only for some objects.
- DISPLAY DOCKING HUD only works in HELM: press F1 before step 7.
- The autopilot does not manage the reactor: keep an eye on ENGINEERING (F2).
- To dock, continue with Dock, repair and resupply from step 6 (DATA LINK).
