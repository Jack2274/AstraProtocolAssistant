INVESTIGATE A PLANET
Scan a planet and launch a probe to look for survivors.

BEFORE YOU START
- Open SCIENCE (F3). The planet must be within short range scanner distance.

STEPS
1. TARGET PLANET              Lock the nearest celestial body
2. SCAN                       Scan the target (or F9)
3. DISPLAY DETAILS            Scientific readout (←/→ pages)
4. LAUNCH PROBE               Send a probe for data the scanner cannot get
5. DISPLAY DETAILS            Read the results again

WATCH
- Each probe is used up; the remaining count is shown in SCIENCE.
- If the signal is noisy: STABILIZE ALL, then FILTER NOISE.
- Report survivors by docking with a station (see Dock, repair and resupply).
