DOCK, REPAIR AND RESUPPLY
Connect to a space station to resupply, repair and report survivors.

STEPS
1. TARGET STATION             Lock the nearest station
2. PLOT ROUTE TARGET          Route to the station
3. ENABLE AUTOPILOT           Approach the station
4. STABILIZE ALL              Stop next to the station
5. DISPLAY DOCKING HUD        Docking view (only in HELM)
6. DATA LINK                  Link with the station (both must accept)
7. REQUEST DOCK               Ask for permission to dock
8. UMBILICAL ANGLE PORT 15    (optional) Umbilical angle, while retracted
9. UMBILICAL EXTEND PORT      Extend the umbilical to the airlock
10. UMBILICAL LOCK PORT       Lock onto the station's airlock
11. OPEN AIRLOCK PORT         Open the passage
12. REQUEST RESUPPLY          Resupply materiel
13. REQUEST DRYDOCK           (if needed) Repair the ship

WATCH
- Use the side (PORT or STARBOARD) facing the station's airlock;
  the DOCKING HUD shows the closest airlock.
- Keep the ship still while locked: too much movement severs the umbilical.
