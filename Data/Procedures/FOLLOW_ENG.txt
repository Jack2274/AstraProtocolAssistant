# keywords: chase, pursue, track, escort, tail, autopilot
FOLLOW A SHIP
Keep chasing a moving ship using the autopilot.

BEFORE YOU START
- The ship must be within short range scanner distance.

STEPS
1. TARGET SHIP                Lock the nearest ship (or FIGHTER for fighters)
2. FOLLOW 100 200             Follow: approach to 100, stop moving at 200
3. ENABLE AUTOPILOT           Start following (or F9 in NAVIGATION)
4. ABORT FOLLOW               Stop following at any time

WATCH
- TARGET SHIP locks the nearest ship, which may be hostile.
- Without arguments FOLLOW uses the game's default distances;
  adjust the values to the target.
- The autopilot does not manage the reactor: keep an eye on ENGINEERING (F2).
