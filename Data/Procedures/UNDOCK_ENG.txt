# keywords: leave station, depart, release, detach, umbilical, airlock
UNDOCK
Leave a station safely after docking.

STEPS
1. CLOSE AIRLOCK PORT         Close the passage (protects life support)
2. UMBILICAL UNLOCK PORT      Release the clamps
3. UMBILICAL RETRACT PORT     Retract the umbilical
4. ABORT LINK                 End the data link
5. SET THRUSTERS -20          Move away sideways (opposite to the station)
6. STABILIZE ALL              Stop at a safe distance

WATCH
- Always UNLOCK before RETRACT, or the umbilical may sever.
- Use the same side (PORT or STARBOARD) used when docking.
