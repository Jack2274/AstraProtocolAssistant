REACTOR EMERGENCY
Bring the reactor temperature down before a meltdown.

STEPS
1. INJECT COOLANT 20          Small dose: cools faster
2. SET LASERS 50              Reduce laser power
3. SET SHIELDS 50             Reduce shield power
4. DISABLE TRACTOR BEAM       Remove the tractor beam load
5. ABORT WARP                 Cancel a warp that is charging
6. INJECT COOLANT 50          Larger dose: cools for longer

WATCH
- Reactor load and temperature are shown in ENGINEERING (F2).
- Coolant is limited: check the stock before injecting.
- Exceeding the maximum temperature destroys the ship.
